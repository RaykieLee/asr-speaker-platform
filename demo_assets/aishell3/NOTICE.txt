This demo contains eight selected test utterances from two distinct AISHELL-3
speaker folders, with their source character/pinyin transcript lines recorded
in source_selection.json and manifest.json. The enrollment WAVs concatenate two
complete utterances from one speaker each. The two-speaker-abab.wav file
concatenates four complete utterances in A/B/A/B order. Preparation inserts
350 ms of zero-valued PCM silence between clips; it does not crop, resample,
change channels, filter, or change gain. The generated files are derived audio
and the exact input IDs, transcript text, source URLs, segment intervals, and
SHA-256 digests are recorded in manifest.json.

AISHELL-3 was released as a multi-speaker Mandarin corpus. The aliases A and B
are demo labels for the selected dataset speaker folders; this package does not
claim to identify the people who recorded them or to establish diarization
accuracy. No additional upstream NOTICE file was present in the selected
Hugging Face dataset root at the pinned revision.
